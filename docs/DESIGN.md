# DiscordX design

The Plugins panel is a compact Android utility. Keep the wordmark, plugin search, and switch in that order at every width. Show what a plugin changes next to its switch. The edge button opens the panel without changing Discord's navigation.

| Role | Dark | Light |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Panel | `#242532` | `#F9F8FF` |
| Control surface | `#313344` | `#EAEAF6` |
| Text | `#F5F3FF` | `#252637` |
| Secondary text | `#C5C6DB` | `#515369` |
| Accent | `#ACB9FD` | `#4D59A3` |

Use Android's system typeface and native switches. Headings use 24 sp, plugin names 17 sp, and supporting text 14 sp. Touch controls are at least 48 dp. The panel caps at 520 dp and scrolls on short windows.
